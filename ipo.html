Two account approaches, compared using the same illustrative entry, position size and starting margin.

Dear Team,

Congratulations to everyone who took part in the IAM campaign. We have completed another important campaign together, and today we can recognise the result with pride.

The comparison below shows how a fixed 1% price target and a 0.2% trailing stop can lead to different gross outcomes under the illustrated price path. The results are calculated before costs and are not a representation of verified individual client results.

Scenario A · Fixed target

+1% price move

$160.00
+100% gross return on starting margin

A 1% rise from the $16.00 entry reaches $16.16. On a $16,000 position, the gross profit is $160 — equal to the $160 starting margin. The starting margin doubles to $320 before costs.

Exit: $16.00 × 1.01 = $16.16
Profit: ($16.16 − $16.00) × 1,000 = $160
Scenario B · 0.2% trailing stop

Exit after a reversal

$3,660.60
+2,287.9% gross return on starting margin

After the initial +1% activation level is reached, the trailing stop follows the running high. Using an illustrative $19.70 peak, a 0.2% pullback gives a calculated stop level of $19.6606.

Stop: $19.70 × (1 − 0.002) = $19.6606
Profit: ($19.6606 − $16.00) × 1,000 = $3,660.60
