# More than 38,000 women and girls killed in Gaza — what the source says

**Published:** 2026-10-08  
**Evidence date:** 2025-12-31  
**Primary source publication:** 2026-04-17  
**Evidence status:** Sourced UN Women estimate

## The finding

UN Women reported that **more than 38,000 women and girls had been killed in Gaza between October 2023 and December 2025**. Its analysis breaks this down as more than 22,000 women and 16,000 girls. UN Women says the actual number of casualties is likely higher because bodies remain under rubble and the collapse of health-information systems constrained documentation. citeturn0search0turn0search6

## Attribution and scope

The UN Women press material describes the deaths as resulting from the war in Gaza; UN Women Chief of Humanitarian Action Sofia Calltorp specifically described them as resulting from Israeli air bombardment and land military operations. citeturn0search1

This article does not convert that source into a broader claim about individual cases, intent, or legal responsibility. Those questions require separate evidence and should be evaluated independently.

## Why the number is important

The figure represents an exceptionally large loss of women and girls over the period covered by the analysis. UN Women also reported nearly 11,000 women and girls with injuries resulting in lifelong disabilities. citeturn0search0
