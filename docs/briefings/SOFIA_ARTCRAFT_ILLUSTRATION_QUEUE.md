# ArtCraft illustration queue — Sofia heritage articles

**Scope:** All articles in the Sofia / Seven Saints heritage series, not only the MindCloud architecture brief.

**Source-of-truth rule:** This queue is a production specification, not a claim that ArtCraft has been run or that all article manuscripts have been located. Match each visual to its actual article before generation; do not invent missing article titles, facts, or dates.

## Per-article deliverables

- One landscape hero illustration (target 16:9).
- One vertical editorial image (target 4:5).
- One explanatory visual (map, timeline, architectural phase diagram, or documented symbol comparison) when warranted by source material.
- Alt text, caption, visual status label, source links, rights/licensing notes, and creation provenance.
- Article-specific ArtCraft project/source file and exported asset reference where supported.

**Sofia — Church of the Seven Saints (Sveti Sedmochislenitsi)**

Art direction: Byzantine-influenced editorial palette (stone, warm gold, midnight blue), architectural realism, readable detail, no invented inscriptions or documentary claims. The church's Ottoman-era predecessor and later church conversion may be represented only in separate, explicitly labeled reconstructions after source verification.

Suggested prompts for human-operated ArtCraft:

**Hero:** “Editorial architectural illustration of the Church of the Seven Saints in Sofia, Bulgaria, from a respectful pedestrian perspective, warm evening light, richly textured masonry, restrained Byzantine-inspired gold and deep-blue grading, elegant publication composition, no text overlays. Reference supplied verified photographs for architectural accuracy. Illustration, not documentary photograph.”

**Architectural explainer:** “Create a clean, source-grounded visual comparison of the building's documented historical phases in Sofia. Distinguish attested features from interpretive reconstruction. Do not add unverified structures, dates, emblems, or inscriptions. Provide a separate legend outside the image.”

**Detail:** “Editorial close-up study of verified church architectural details from supplied reference photographs, natural materials and historically grounded ornament, no invented saints or inscriptions.”
