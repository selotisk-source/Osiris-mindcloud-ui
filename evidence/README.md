# MindCloud Journal

A public, source-labelled archive of research, evidence records, investigations, and project history.

## Editorial rules

- Separate sourced facts from interpretation and hypotheses.
- Preserve publisher, publication date, source URL, and relevant limitations.
- Do not present a MindCloud transcription or synthesis as an independent measurement.
- Do not publish private identifying or security-sensitive information.
- Where evidence is disputed or estimated, label it explicitly.
