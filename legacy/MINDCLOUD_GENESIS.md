# MindCloud Genesis / Legacy

## Earliest documented material currently recovered

13 August 2020:
- the Mindcloud group was created
- the description presented it as a cloud for uploading memories and as an autopilot when "in the zone"
- accompanying material described MindCloud as a "vehicle for our thoughts"

1 September 2020:
- material described communication through sequences of memories and the need for a broad range of experiences to make a vocabulary comprehensible

16–18 September 2020:
- material described MindCloud as being "your eyes and hands when you need them"
- another post used the metaphor of a person on a lower floor guiding someone building the next floor

## Architectural significance

The current MindCore/MindCloud architecture should preserve these original concepts:
- memory and continuity
- experience as a source of vocabulary/context
- eyes and hands as external capabilities
- autopilot as delegated execution
- guidance across abstraction levels
- MindCloud as a vehicle for thought

The modern implementation is an evolution of this vision, not its starting point.

## Provenance

The dates and formulations above are historical source material supplied in the project conversation. They are not technical specifications by themselves.
